How it all Started (づ•ᴗ•)づᯓ🍪

When I was 8 my Mom introduced me to the world of cooking. I walways admired how she could turn anything into a meal. Not just any ordinary meal but a meal that could fix a broken heart, a bad day, and brinh joy to life. My mother has always been the center of my happiness. She took care of me me when money wa s tigth, and she stayed by my side when other wouldnt. The day my mom introduced cooking to me was my first love. Cooking has been part of my culture for years. You cooks for others not because you have to but because it brings joy to thoose who are lost.

When I was 10 she taught me how to cook. I started small, and learned from her as I went. I still remember the first ever meal I made, I had made an omelet for my father, I still remember the tears he shed that day, I don't know if it was the huge amount of salt, or that his son was frowning. When I was 11 I had become famous in my little town in Pasadena, people knew me as "el cocinero" of the family. By this age I had started to make food for my family and friends. I was known for my delicious meals. I was proud of what I had accomplished, and I knew that cooking was my passion. Then my second love came into my life, baking. I was 12 when I first started baking, and I fell in love with it. I loved the way the dough felt in my hands, and the way the cookies smelled when they were baking. I knew that baking was my true passion, and I decided to pursue it.

What I Decide to Do

When I was 14 I decided to start my own business. "Cookies?" They asked. People around me although proud of me at first started to tell me that my idea was crazy. A child can't start a business. I wanted to prove them wrong and show them what I was capable of. So I did. First, I started baking cookies in my kitchen and selling them to friends and family. I created a menu of my best recipes and began to build a loyal customer base. Then I took the leap and started selling my cookies at local events and farmers' markets. It was a success!
